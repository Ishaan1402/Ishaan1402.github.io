Research

High-Performance Data Structures for Dynamic Graph Workloads

During my undergraduate research at UNC Charlotte's Data Intelligence Research Laboratory, I specialized in optimizing high-performance data structures for dynamic graph workloads.

My work focused on implementing and optimizing Microsoft's ALEX learned index and the industry-leading STX B+ tree using C++. I accelerated graph analysis algorithms including PageRank and Breadth First Search through multithreaded implementations.

Limitations

  • Not Field Deployed: BuoyNet is an IoT evaluation and viability study. It has not been tested in open water or on a physical buoy.
  • Simulated Edge Metrics: Latency and energy numbers are not yet benchmarked on hardware. They are modeled from Colab timing benchmarks and scaled for Raspberry Pi 4B.
  • Synthetic Domain Shift: Robustness stress tests use transformations replicating turbidity, biofouling, and dim lighting on held-out splits rather than real underwater streams.
