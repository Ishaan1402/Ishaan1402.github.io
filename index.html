A live app that pulls from 11 sources, including AirNow monitors, weather, NASA FIRMS fire hotspots and satellite aerosol data, to rank the likely causes of a local air quality spike. crack-seg

Finds cracks in bridge concrete from drone photos

A custom U-Net with sliding-window inference over high-resolution UAV imagery, so thin cracks stay visible without blowing GPU memory. Overlapping tiles are blended with a Gaussian weight map; the current checkpoint adds squeeze-and-excitation and deep supervision.
